Highlights

• Three machine-learned potentials are audited on Cu, Ni and Fe boundary energies

• Topology alone blind-predicts intergranular percolation at AUC 0.77 (CI 0.62–0.90)

• A decoupled control shows part of the energy advantage is built in by the damage rule

• A phase-field cross-check reproduces the ranking direction but bounds the rule

• Machine-learning error is absorbed, not amplified, along the energy-to-failure chain
