Cover letter — International Journal of Plasticity

Dear Editor,

We would like to submit our manuscript entitled "Grain-boundary network
percolation meets machine-learned grain-boundary energies: blind prediction of
intergranular failure susceptibility" for consideration as an original
research paper in the International Journal of Plasticity.

This is a plasticity-informed, not plasticity-mechanistic, study: CPFEM supplies
the boundary-normal stress histories, and the contribution is the audited
composition of MLIP energies, percolation topology and a bounded failure label.
The closest IJP antecedent is Zhang et al. (2022, 150, 103186), which predicts
per-boundary damage sensitivity; we extend that line to network-level percolative
failure with grouped blind testing and an independent phase-field bound. We
deliberately do not claim validation: the phase-field cross-check bounds the label
(median ρ 0.23; 22–38% arrest of rule-percolated cases), and all claims are framed
as relative rankings under a fixed synthetic rule.

The concrete mechanics checks behind that framing are:

- The labels come from an equivalent-damage rule, not from a fracture-mechanics
  criterion. We therefore re-solve a 46-microstructure subset with an independent
  phase-field fracture engine (AT2, 138 runs) that is given nothing but the same
  boundary-energy map. It is directionally consistent with the ordering, but it
  arrests 22–38% of the cases the rule labels as percolated: the equivalent-damage
  rule is systematically more permissive.
- A decoupled control isolates how much of the energy channel's predictive
  advantage is constructed by the damage rule rather than discovered from the
  microstructure.
- Uncertainty is quantified end to end: microstructure-grouped cross-validation,
  split-conformal intervals, a k-nearest-neighbour applicability domain, and an
  error-propagation audit whose perturbation source is the measured spread between
  three machine-learned potentials rather than an assumed noise model.

All data, labels and code (including the phase-field engine) are released with the
paper, so the failure label itself can be re-derived and challenged.

We confirm that this manuscript is original, has not been published previously,
and is not under consideration by another journal. All authors have approved the
manuscript and agree with its submission. The authors declare no competing
financial interests or personal relationships (statement included in the
manuscript), and a CRediT authorship contribution statement is provided.
No preprint version of this manuscript is available.
