Cover letter — npj Computational Materials

Dear Editors,

We would like to submit our manuscript entitled "Grain-boundary network
percolation meets machine-learned grain-boundary energies: blind prediction
of intergranular failure susceptibility" for
consideration as an Article in npj Computational Materials.

To our knowledge, no validated pipeline currently connects boundary-energy
spectra, network topology, and failure susceptibility end to end. Our
manuscript presents a fully computational, auditable pipeline that composes
machine-learned grain-boundary energies, percolation descriptors of periodic
microstructure networks, crystal-plasticity-derived failure labels, and a
microstructure-grouped blind test with conformal uncertainty quantification
and an error-propagation audit. Key points of interest for the journal's
readership include:

- A leakage-controlled, fully blind evaluation on twenty unseen
  microstructures (twelve added after the model was frozen), every headline
  metric reported with a microstructure-group bootstrap confidence interval.
- A decoupled control showing which part of the model's energy-channel
  advantage is constructed by the label rule rather than discovered — an
  honest decomposition of topology versus energy contributions.
- An error-propagation audit driven by the measured inter-potential spread
  of machine-learned potentials, finding that predictions absorb rather than
  amplify atomic-scale error.
- An independent cross-check with a from-scratch phase-field fracture
  engine (46 microstructures, 138 runs) that knows nothing about the
  equivalent-damage rule: it recovers the direction of the susceptibility
  ordering and quantifies how much more permissive the synthetic label is
  than a fracture-mechanics criterion, bounding rather than overselling the
  label.

This work is methodological in positioning: it reports relative failure
susceptibility rankings under a fixed, documented equivalent-damage rule,
with quantified uncertainty throughout.

We confirm that this manuscript is original, has not been published
previously, and is not under consideration by another journal. All authors
have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission. The authors
declare no competing interests (statement included in the manuscript).
No preprint version of this manuscript is available.
